# Help and Support
GeoLab is a growing platform and community. Our documentation is currently limited in scope, yet we value your questions and feedback. We want to help you be successful and leverage the platform to its full capacity. Chances are, if you're stuck, someone else is too &mdash; please ask! Your questions will help us evolve our documentation and resource offerings to best support the community's needs.

We offer a number of help avenues. Selecting the appropriate option for your specific need will ensure the most expedient response time. 

## Resource Request Form
If you need increased access to GeoLab resources (i.e., larger instance sizes, GPU access for machine learning workflows, or increased resource quotas) please fill out the {{ resource_request_form }}. A member of our team will review your request within two business days.

Please note that GeoLab is being offered to the community as a free service, paid for by grant funds to EarthScope from the National Science Foundation. Access to increase compute resources is restricted in order to control costs. Please prototype your analyses on smaller servers before requesting more resources, and justify your request. In some cases, we may encourage you to rethink your computational approach in order to use resources more efficiently.

## Discourse Forum
Join us on Discourse! Launching June 2026, this community forum is designed to facilitate conversations amongst the community. 
This resource is most effective for the following types of support:
- learning about software updates to GeoLab, configuration changes, outage announcements, etc 
- sharing your notebooks and research conversationally with the broader geophysics community
- soliciting help from other domain experts within the community, such as:
    - identifying and accessing appropriate datasets for your work
    - troubleshooting your code or unexpected results
    - questions about cloud performance optimization for specific research tasks
- providing your insight, expertise, and mentorship to other GeoLab users

Please note that this forum is public. It is the best avenue for questions that might be relevant to others in the geosciences, and/or can be answered by peers in the research community. For support from a staff adminstrator, please use email the help desk. 

Log in to Discourse HERE using your EarthScope User Account.

## Help Desk
We offer a traditional email support ticket system. Please email us at help@earthscope.org if you need individual assistance from GeoLab administrators.
This path is most effective for the following types of problems:
- addressing individual issues with account creation, lost passwords, and other access issues
- problems with server launches, crashes, and technical infrastructure bug reports that require GeoLab administrator intervention (please check Discourse first to see if others are reporting similar problems)
Please note that EarthScope admins are IT professionals, not research scientists. For support with individual research code debugging, please use the appropriate community support threads on Discourse. 

## We welcome your feedback! 
Please [email us](help) or reach out on Discourse if you'd like to provide immediate feedback on the platform. We also conduct regular user feedback surveys, so keep your eye on your inbox for opportunities to help shape the future of the platform.
