# Examples and Tutorials
EarthScope is in the process of building out a resource library of examples, tutorials, and educational resources to help you adapt your existing workflows to the cloud, and to grow your scientific computing skills. Please explore these resources and we appreciate your patience as we gradually develop this material.

## Cloud Foundations
This free, asynchronous online course is designed to orient you to the GeoLab platform and the why's of computing in the cloud. In 2-3 hours, it will walk you through the GeoLab login, get you started on your first project, and ground you in the fundamentals of working in a JupyterHub. 

To get started, use your EarthScope User Account to log in to our [online learning platform](cloud_foundations). Select Cloud Foundations under the Open Enrollment section. 

You'll earn a micro-credential upon completion, which you can display on your LinkedIn or highlight on a CV. 

## GeoLab Learning Hub
Topic-specific examples and tutorials are located in the [GeoLab Learning Hub](learning_hub), a github repository dedicated to educational notebooks and learning materials. 

We recommend you [use `git`](./using_git.md) to clone or fork this entire repository into your workspace, so you have your own copy of the notebook files to work on.  

## Short Courses and Workshops
EarthScope offers a number of in-depth short courses and workshops on specific topics in geophysics. Many of these operate in GeoLab and teach scientific computing concepts. Check out the offerings and register [here](course_offerings).
